# Vyora — Quick Start

> For the merchant. Five minutes. Keep this open on your phone the first day.

Vyora replaces your udhaar notebook. Everything stays **on your phone** — no account, no internet
needed, nobody else can see it.

---

## 1. Put Vyora on your home screen

1. Open **esytol.com/vyora** in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install Vyora** when the box appears.
3. The Vyora icon appears with your other apps. Open it from there from now on.

**iPhone:** tap the **Share** button in Safari → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.

## 2. Record your first credit

Tap the blue **＋ Credit** button.

1. **Amount** — the keypad is already open. Type it, or tap ₹100 / ₹500 / ₹1000 / ₹2000.
2. **Party** — type the customer's name. If they are new, Vyora creates them. No separate step.
3. **Pay back in** — tap **30 days** (or 7, 15, 45). Vyora shows _"Due 31 Aug · Sunday"_ so you know
   the real date.
4. **Save** — or **Save & add another** to keep going.

That is the whole thing. Under ten seconds once you have done it twice.

## 3. Record a payment

Tap the green **＋ Payment**.

- **They paid me** — money coming in (the usual case).
- **I paid them** — money you paid a supplier.

Same three fields. The balance updates immediately.

## 4. See who owes you

**Home** shows your position: _You'll get_, _You'll pay_, and today's cash.

## 5. Know who to call first — **Chase**

Tap **Chase** in the bottom bar.

Everyone who owes you, **most important first**. Each row shows how much, how late, their phone
number, when you last spoke to them, and what to do next.

- Tap the **phone number** to call.
- Tap **What to say** to see a ready-made message. Choose **gentle**, **normal** or **firm**.
- Tap **Share reminder** to send it from your own WhatsApp.

**Vyora never messages anyone.** You send it, from your phone, in your name.

## 6. Finish the day — **Closing**

**More → Closing**, at the end of trade.

One screen: money collected today, credit given, cash paid out, and **who is due tomorrow**. Add a
note if you want. Tap **Finish today**.

Thirty seconds. Do it daily and you will always know where you stand.

## 7. Keep your book safe — **Backup**

**More → Settings.**

The top of the screen tells you plainly: **Your data is backed up** ✅, **Backup recommended** ⚠️, or
**Never backed up** ❌ — and how many entries would be lost.

Tap **Export ledger**. A file saves to your phone. **Send that file to yourself on WhatsApp.** That
is your safe copy.

Do this on day one, then once a week.

## 8. Get your book back — **Restore**

New phone, or something went wrong:

1. **More → Settings → Restore from backup** → choose your file.
2. Vyora shows exactly what is in it — how many customers, credits, payments — **before** anything
   changes.
3. Tap **Restore this file**.

Restore **replaces** everything currently in the app. If the phone already has entries you want,
export those first.

## Common mistakes

**"I lost my phone and lost everything."**
Your book lives only on that phone. Export weekly and send the file to yourself. This is the one
habit that matters.

**"I typed the wrong amount."**
Open the customer from **Parties**, find the entry, tap **✕** to delete it, then record it again.

**"Two customers have the same name."**
Vyora will treat them as one person. Add something to tell them apart — _Ramesh Kaka_, _Ramesh
Tailor_ — or add a phone number.

**"I skipped the due date."**
Then Chase can only tell you an entry is _old_, not _late_. One tap on **30 days** is what makes
Chase useful.

**"Nothing was saved" message appeared.**
Your phone is out of space and the entry was **not** recorded. Export a backup, free up space, then
enter it again. Vyora will never tell you something saved when it did not.

**"I cleared my browser data."**
That erases Vyora too. Restore from your exported file.

---

**Your data never leaves your phone.** Not to us, not to anyone. That also means only you can keep
it safe — so please export weekly.
